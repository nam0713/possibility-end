# v9.1 — owner feedback (2026-10-13) and contracts

The owner reviewed v9 and asked for four changes. They override design/V9-BRIEF.md where they conflict.

1. **One theme colour.** The orange/ember/amber accent mixed with ice cyan is disliked. Unify everything —
   UI, 3D formations, accents and the ending film — into ONE cold hue family. No orange, amber, gold, red,
   pink, yellow or green anywhere (only exception: none). Physical objects follow the palette too
   (a blue-white star instead of an orange sun, an ice-white accretion disc, a white-hot → ice-cyan → deep-blue
   birth instead of orange, a blue planet with white clouds and slate/blue-grey land).
2. **Reading = scattered stars.** Formations ("objects") appear on chapter covers and home sections only.
   When the reader scrolls from the chapter cover into the body text, the formation's particles **scatter** into
   a sparse, calm starfield that fills the viewport; scrolling back up **re-gathers** them into the formation; at
   the chapter end they gather into the next chapter's formation (ch.8: into the waiting seed). The motion must
   feel like matter dispersing and re-attaching — continuous and reversible with scroll, never a cut.
3. **Ending entry: exactly one light.** At the end of ch.8 the only light is the universe's seed. Today a second
   light appears (an HTML star/glow near the seed, and a big HTML bloom when pressed). Remove every HTML glow;
   the seed itself answers hover/focus and, on press, the seed itself flares and becomes the collapse core —
   one continuous light from the waiting seed into the film.
4. **Dark mode only.** Remove the 종이 / 밝게 themes entirely (UI, CSS, storage, tests, docs). Saved
   'paper'/'light' preferences are silently dropped.

## Contract additions (window.Universe)

```js
Universe.setScatter(v)      // 0 = formation (cover/object), 1 = fully scattered starfield (reading body). Called by the
                            // UI on every scroll frame in the reader; the engine eases toward the target (damped) so
                            // scroll jitter never shows. Per-particle staggered thresholds + flow-noise displacement so
                            // stars peel away in streams and re-attach in streams. Fully reversible. Works on top of
                            // morphs: if the scene changes while scattered (next-chapter handover), the stars gather
                            // into the NEW formation. In the scattered state only a sparse subset stays visible
                            // (a calm starfield, not noise behind text); the rest fade out and fade back in on gather.
                            // Reduced motion: no travel — a cross-fade between formation and starfield.
                            // getState().scatter reports the current (eased) value. No-op when WebGL is unavailable.
Universe.ending.arm(bool)   // hover/focus on the ch.8 trigger: the seed brightens/breathes a little (no-op elsewhere).
```
Engine → film handover adds `focus: [x, y, z]` (world position of the seed / collapse point) to
`film.begin({...})`. The film collapses EXACTLY onto `focus`, and its first frames continue the seed's own light
(same screen position, size and brightness) — no second light, no centroid offset. If `focus` is absent the
film keeps its old centroid estimate.

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-13, owner): three.js colour is free — make it physically right

The owner gave full freedom over colour in the three.js parts (orange included) and is still unhappy with the
palette-driven result — e.g. the cooling young Earth reads lilac, then like a grey moon. New principle, replacing the
four-hue palette for the universe (the UI keeps its single ice accent):

- **Thermal light follows a blackbody (Planck) ramp by temperature** — dull red → orange → yellow → white → faintly
  blue-white for the hottest: the birth fireball, accretion discs (Doppler-beamed: approaching side brighter/bluer),
  stars (by class: red dwarfs, sun-like yellow-white, hot blue-white), supernovae, lava. Use a proper Kelvin→linear
  sRGB function, not hand-picked hexes.
- **Gas and dust use real astrophotography colours** — H-alpha pink-red and O-III teal for emission, blue reflection,
  brown-grey dust lanes, yellowish old bulges, blue young arms, pink HII knots, a neutral-blue cosmic web.
- **The young Earth cools like a real planet**: molten orange-yellow surface with glowing cracks → dark basalt crust
  with dimming red fissures → steam and cloud cover → blue oceans, white clouds, brown-green-grey land, thin blue
  atmospheric rim. "작고 푸른 행성" at the end.
- **Abstract formations** (cover ring, ch.1–4) keep ice/white as structure; warmth appears only where something
  emits (cores, nodes, the seed: white-hot). The UI's ice ties everything together.
- Keep: exactly one light at the ending entry; the scatter; the timing contract; restraint and beauty over saturation.
